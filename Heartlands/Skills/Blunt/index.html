Blunt

Blunt arms, clubs, hammers, and maces have endured across ages not for their grace, but for their certainty. They do not slice or pierce; they crush.

Usage

Blunt weapons like clubs, maces, and hammers can be used one- or two-handed depending on their size.

Perks

  • Pulverize: Lvl 10, slight slow to targets in heavy armor after hit.
  • Kneecapper: Lvl 20, slow enemies by hitting them in their legs.
  • Good Bones: Lvl 30, take less damage from blunt weapons.
  • Inertia Blows: Lvl 40, player weight increases damage with blunt weapons.
  • Staggering Blow: Lvl 50, when you hit an enemy, 20% chance to apply an effect that makes their attacks 30% weaker.
  • First Impression: Lvl 60, A low chance to ragdoll enemies when hitting them with blunt weapon and 25% to demoralize group.
  • Shatter Strike: Lvl 70, Blunt attacks deal very high durability damage.
  • Shield Breaker: Lvl 80, 15% chance to instantly break shields.
  • Blunt Force Trauma: Lvl 90, When hitting an enemy in the head with a blunt weapon, 10% chance to instantly kill them.
  • Concussion: Lvl 100, Chance to lower all enemy character skills when hitting them in the head with a blunt weapon.
  • Echo Chamber: Lvl 110, Hitting enemy with a blunt weapon while they wear heavy armor ignores 50% of their armor.
  • Home Run: Lvl 120, Killing an enemy with a blunt weapon sends them flying.
  • Loot Crate: Lvl 125, Chance to remove npc inventory when hitting with hammer.
