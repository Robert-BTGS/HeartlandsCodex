Usage

Light Armor offers less protection than heavy armor but keeps you nimble.

Perks

  • Fleet: Lvl 10, Wearing a full set of light armor reduces damage by 5% when moving.
  • Light Foot: Lvl 20, While wearing light armor boots, your foot steps are 25 percent quieter than usual.
  • Velvet Gloves: Lvl 30, 15% less chance of being detected while pickpocketing and wearing light gloves.
  • Staunch Leather: Lvl 40, Light armor gains resistance to bleed effects.
  • Trap Card: Lvl 50, After taking the first damage in a combat encounter you move 10 percent faster for 5 seconds.
  • Oiled Leathers: Lvl 60, Light armor loses durability 20 percent slower.
  • Embroidered Runework: Lvl 70, Light armor enchantments are 5% more effective.
  • Custom Fit: Lvl 80, Full set of light armor has no weight.
  • Adaptive Materials: Lvl 90, While wearing light armor Chameleon spells are 10 percent more effective.
  • Mana Weave: Lvl 100, Light armor no longer has any effect on spell effectiveness.
  • Ebb…: Lvl 110, Ranged damage received is reduced by 35% when wearing a full set of light armor.
  • …and Flow: Lvl 120, Outgoing ranged damage is increased by 25% when wearing full set of light armor.
  • Momentum: Lvl 125, For each piece of light armor worn, gain a 10% damage boost.
