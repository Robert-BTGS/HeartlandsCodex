Enchanting

Enchanting is the power to use magic on wordly objects, enhancing those objects power to use in battle.

Perks

  • Arcane Studies: Lvl 10, Enchant bench runes last slightly longer making enchanting easier.
  • Recyclomancy: Lvl 20, When enchanting items you have a chance to have your heart shard just become depleted instead of being destroyed entirely.
  • Mending Energies: Lvl 30, Can repair enchanted item 15% when replenishing enchantment with a shard.
  • Twinned Enchantment: Lvl 40, Apply two enchantments to an item.
  • Enchanter’s Guard: Lvl 50, Recharging a weapon mid combat triggers a time dilation with strength and duration equivalent to your enchant skill as if it were your block skill.
  • Magical Overload: Lvl 60, After you recharge a weapon its next hit does 25 percent more damage.
  • Mental Anchoring: Lvl 70, when enchanting attribute based enchantments they scale based on your current level of that attribute 10 to 20 percent stronger.
  • Arcane Triplicate: Lvl 80, Apply three enchantments to an item.
  • Triquetric Formulas: Lvl 90, If an enchantment uses three separate schools of magic it uses 40 percent less charge per hit.
  • One Final Effort: Lvl 100, When an enchanted weapon is depleted the next hit has a chance to release an overcharged AOE effect based on player’s magic. Magic is drained when this happens.
  • Divine Conduit: Lvl 110, If you hold two heart shards you can recharge any holstered weapons; the heart shards charge is divided amongst the number of holstered gear.
  • Glorious Ascension: Lvl 120, A piece of armor you enchant with 2 effects has a 25% chance to gain a third effect of either absorb magic or reflect damage.
  • Matrix of Eternity: Lvl 125, Apply a single permanent enchantment to a weapon.
