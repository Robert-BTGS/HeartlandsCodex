Block

Block is the art of discipline under pressure—the moment between chaos, and control. Shields become more than barriers; they are vows sworn in silence, stances held against storms.

Usage

Shields come in three sizes. Bucklers are small and light, standard shields offer solid protection, and great shields are large, heavy, and very protective. See Shield Types for details.

Perks

  • Weightless Ward: Lvl 10, shields weigh less when carried.
  • Defensive Maintenance: Lvl 20, shields lose durability slower.
  • Punisher: Lvl 30, deal a slight percentage of attack back to the attacker when blocking.
  • Sundering Shield: Lvl 40, 10% chance to remove 10% weapon durability when weapon hits a shield.
  • Retaliation: Lvl 50, gain bonus damage after parrying an attack.
  • Focused: Lvl 60, increases the chances of time dilation triggering.
  • Backlash: Lvl 70, chance to disarm on successful block.
  • Subvert: Lvl 80, 20% chance to deflect arrow towards shooter on parry.
  • Showstopper: Lvl 90, chance to briefly paralyze target on shield bash.
  • Turtle: Lvl 100, shields holstered on back add to your armor.
  • Offensive Bulwark: Lvl 110, bucklers have chance to paralyze.
  • Sword Breaker: Lvl 120, 5% chance to shatter enemy weapon on parry.
  • Aegis: Lvl 125, after parry, invulnerable to next physical hit.
