Alchemy

Alchemy represents your ability to craft potent potions and putrid poisons. It relies on Intelligence, and the application of trial and error.

Perks

  • Supplements: Lvl 10, primary effects of ingredients you consume last very slightly longer.
  • Best Bits: Lvl 20, potions and poisons you craft are 20% stronger.
  • Herb Lore: Lvl 30, learn 2nd effect for all ingredients.
  • Alchemist's Pouch: Lvl 40, alchemical ingredients have no weight.
  • Stable Compounds: Lvl 50, potions and poisons you craft are 40% stronger.
  • Herbal Mastery: Lvl 60, learn all 3 effects of all ingredients.
  • Ampoule Band: Lvl 70, potions the player makes have no weight.
  • Distilling Process: Lvl 80, when making a buff potion, remove negative effect. When making poison, remove positive effect.
  • Potent Potions: Lvl 90, potions and poisons you craft are 60% stronger.
  • Byproduct: Lvl 100, you create a duplicate potion when using an alchemy station.
  • Transmutation: Lvl 110, you can create a potion with a single ingredient.
  • Flux Brewing: Lvl 120, potions you make have a slight chance of to refund one ingredient.
  • Grand Transmuter: Lvl 125, you create two identical potions when using an alchemy station.
