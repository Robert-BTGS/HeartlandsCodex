Staff

Use of a staff to channel the arcane takes no small measure of care and focus, and the power you will wield is unmatched.

Usage

Staves cast powerful placement spells (such as pits of fire or lightning from the sky). They rely on a stored charge that must be replenished with a Heart Shard when depleted.

Perks

  • Font of Power: Lvl 10, Spells have a 10% larger radius.
  • Catalyst: Lvl 20, Staves deal bonus melee damage to creatures with active magical effects from the staff.
  • Dampened Limiters: Lvl 30, Can cast 25% farther away.
  • Suppression Field: Lvl 40, Enemies hit with staff spells are silenced while inside their radius.
  • Arcane Battery: Lvl 50, Staves use less of their energy to cast.
  • Overclocking: Lvl 60, If you wield two staves of the same type of damage their strength is doubled.
  • Recharge: Lvl 70, Chance to regain staff energy when Catalyst triggers.
  • Efficient Channels: Lvl 80, Very slight chance for staff to not consume staff’s energy.
  • Reserve Power: Lvl 90, Spells cast through staves are strengthened by your skill in their governing school.
  • Lightning Rod: Lvl 100, Regain spell charges when hitting creatures with active spell effects.
  • Ablative Field: Lvl 110, When inside the area of a placement spell cast through a staff, gain a shield corresponding to that spell.
  • Echoes: Lvl 120, after a spell effect ends you have a 20 percent chance for a weakened version of the same spell to appear at the same location 2 seconds later.
  • Event Horizon: Lvl 125, Staff spells track the first enemy they hit for the duration of their effect.
