Repair

Keep the cracks in your armor and chips in your blade from sundering in battle. Perhaps you will solve the riddle of steel.

Perks

  • Maintenance: Lvl 10, Equipment weight scales inversely to equipment durability.
  • Sewing Kit: Lvl 20, Clothing passively repairs to 80% in inventory.
  • Smithy I: Lvl 30, Can fully repair equipment to 100%.
  • Grindstone 1: Lvl 40, Repaired weapons deal +10% damage for first 10 hits.
  • Magewrite: Lvl 50, Can repair enchanted equipment.
  • Smithy II: Lvl 60, Can repair equipment to 110% durability.
  • Grindstone 2: Lvl 70, Repaired weapons deal +10% damage for first 30 hits.
  • Smithy III: Lvl 80, Can repair equipment to 120% durability.
  • Artisan Tools: Lvl 90, Light Armor repairs passively to 82.7% while in inventory.
  • Grindstone 3: Lvl 100, Repaired weapons deal +15% damage for first 50 hits.
  • Smithy IV: Lvl 110, Can repair equipment to 130% durability.
  • Nice Repairs: Lvl 120, Heavy Armor repairs passively to 69.42069% while in inventory.
  • Smithy V: Lvl 125, Can repair equipment to 150% durability.
