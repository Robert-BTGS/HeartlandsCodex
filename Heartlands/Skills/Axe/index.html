Axe

The axe is not a weapon of finesse, it is a tool of finality. Each swing carries the weight of harvest and war alike, cleaving wood and bone in twain. There is no elegance in its arc, only intention.

Usage

Axes come in a couple shapes. Some work fine with one hand, while others require two hands to get full effectiveness.

Perks

  • Woodsman: Lvl 10, deal slightly more damage to plants and beasts when using axes.
  • Scarred: Lvl 20, you take 10% less damage from axes.
  • Bloodlust: Lvl 30, you deal 6% bonus damage for every 1% of your missing health.
  • Jagged Edge: Lvl 40, 50% chance to apply bleed to enemies when using axes.
  • Rending Strikes: Lvl 50, attacks with axes deplete more item health from armor.
  • Bloodfury: Lvl 60, you deal 12% bonus damage for every 1% of your missing health.
  • Serrated Edge: Lvl 70, bleeding applied with axes last 50% longer.
  • Slightly Rusty: Lvl 80, 10-40% chance to poison based on axe durability.
  • Bloodrage: Lvl 90, you deal bonus damage to injured enemies while weilding axes.
  • Executioner's Cleave: Lvl 100, 15% chance to deal 225% damage to enemies when striking their head with an axe.
  • Seam Ripper: Lvl 110, hitting enemy with an axe while they wear light armor ignores 50% of their armor.
  • Reaper's Strike: Lvl 120, you gain massive bonus damage after killing an enemy with an axe.
  • Sanguine's Fury: Lvl 125, you gain 50% of bleed damage you inflict with axes.
