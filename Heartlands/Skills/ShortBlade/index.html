Short Blade

Agile and quick you know how to exploit every opening in armor and form. Short swords and daggers cut deeper in your hands.

Usage

Short Blade covers daggers and short swords. These weapons are always one-handed and tend to be quicker and more nimble than larger blades.

Perks

  • Weak point: Lvl 10, Deal 10% more damage with short blades during time dilation.
  • Responsive: Lvl 20, When only wielding a single short sword you gain 5 percent more time during time dilation.
  • Trusty blade: Lvl 30, short blades you have repaired yourself degrade 10% slower and do 5% more damage.
  • Veil of Blades: Lvl 40, Slight bonus to armor when using two short blades.
  • Boot Knife: Lvl 50, Access to additional weapon slots for short blades on player boots.
  • Chakra Slice: Lvl 60, Apply magic bleed to enemies.
  • One more trick: Lvl 70, tier 3 spells.
  • Riposte: Lvl 80, Percentage chance for player to enter time dilation when NPC would otherwise block/parry their attack based on players short blade skill.
  • Blur: Lvl 90, There is no maximum radius for time dilation.
  • Mirror finish: Lvl 100, You have a chance to deflect projectile spells with your sword.
  • Mythic Strike: Lvl 110, Slicing someones ankles while undetected give you a 20% chance to ragdoll them.
  • Blade dancer: Lvl 120, if you are dual wielding short swords and moving at full speed you do 30% extra damage.
  • Jugular Strike: Lvl 125, If you hit an enemy in heavy armor in the neck you have a 80% chance to instantly kill them.
