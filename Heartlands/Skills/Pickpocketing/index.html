Pickpocketing

Pickpocketing is the art of silently lifting valuables, tools, or key items from unsuspecting targets. Whether it’s slipping a coin pouch from a distracted noble or lifting a cell key from a guard, pickpocketing requires finesse, timing, and a good exit plan. In the right hands, it's a tool for survival, sabotage, or just a little extra coin on the side.

Perks

  • Light Fingers, Lvl 10, +10% success rate on all pickpocket attempts.
  • Deep Pockets, Lvl 20, Unlocks ability to steal items up to 25 gold.
  • Slippery Escape, Lvl 30, Gain a temporary movement speed boost after a failed attempt.
  • Inside Job, Lvl 40, +25% success when disguised.
  • Hands of Fate, Lvl 50, Chance to gain a temporary buff of either speed, luck, or strength, after a successful pickpocket.
  • Crowd Ghost, Lvl 60, Pickpocketing in crowds is 50% easier, and detection delay is doubled.
  • Pressure Points, Lvl 70, Pickpocketing chance increases by 15% on targets wearing heavy armor or bulky clothing.
  • Magpie’s Instinct, Lvl 80, Highlights high-value items in NPC inventories; +10% to steal rare or enchanted items.
  • Misdirection, Lvl 90, Temporarily distracts a target, lowering their awareness for 5 seconds before the attempt.
  • Silent Snatch, Lvl 100, Reduces the chance of a target noticing an item is missing until they try to use it or check their inventory.
  • Spider’s Touch, Lvl 110, Pickpocketing now works during stealth takedowns, letting you steal key items mid-ambush.
  • Cutpurse's Edge, Lvl 120, Gain a small amount of health each time you successfully pickpocket, scaling with item value.
  • Master Thief, Lvl 125, Guaranteed success on pickpocket attempts of common items and minor magical trinkets.
