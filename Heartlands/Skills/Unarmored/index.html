Unarmored

Avoid and evade incoming blows and never be unprepared for battle in any outfit.

Perks

  • Free Movement: Lvl 10, Reduce player noise while unarmored.
  • Built Tough: Lvl 20, Gain 10% increase to armor value based off endurance.
  • Stamina: Lvl 30, Slightly faster health regeneration.
  • Fast Reflexes: Lvl 40, Enemies are less likely to hit you with projectiles.
  • Cosmic Energy: Lvl 50, Slightly faster magic regeneration.
  • Unburdened: Lvl 60, Time dilation triggers more often.
  • Built Saloen Tough: Lvl 70, Gain 20% increase to armor value based off endurance.
  • Calloused: Lvl 80, Take 50% less damage from non-weapon atttacks.
  • Tunnel Vision: Lvl 90, When below 50% health damage taken is reduced by 20 percent and movement speed is increased by 10%.
  • Built Fjord Tough: Lvl 100, Gain 50% increase to armor based off endurance.
  • Resistant Chakras: Lvl 110, Magic resistance increased by 25%.
  • Iron Skin: Lvl 120, There is a 50% chance for arrows to ricochet off of you while unarmored.
  • Special boy: Lvl 125, You gain immunity to bleed, poison, and paralysis.
