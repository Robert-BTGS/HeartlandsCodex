Khurvann (Khurvak)

The Bleeding North

  • Capital: Dravosk
  • Dominant Race: Usai
  • Landscape: Taiga, glacial rivers, frost‑covered steppe

Essence

Stark and brutal, Khurvann remembers a thousand scars. Home to the Usai, the wolf‑blooded, it is a land forged in war and ice. Here, clan loyalties are blood‑bound, and the old songs are sung in growls and roars. They speak often of the Great Silence—a mythic hush before the world’s end. Yet for all their violence, the Usai hold deep honor. When they fight, they fight as storm incarnate. When they swear fealty, it is eternal.
