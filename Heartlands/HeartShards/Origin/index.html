Origin of Heart Shards

The Shattering of Divinity

When Oris, the First, fractured into Vaoris and Eloris, their clash birthed not just time and form, but the tension between them. This divine conflict culminated in the Mythic Wars, where the Adren and Didren, once thoughtforms known as the Dren, embodied themselves and waged battle across the planes. See The Primordials and Gods.

In the final cataclysm of that war, when the Adren sacrificed much of their essence to seal the Didren into Mythara, shards of their divine will and emotion were cast into the mortal plane. These became Heart Shards: fragments of thought, feeling, and raw purpose, solidified by the intensity of that final sacrifice.

Some claim Thirel, the third moon, is the greatest of these: the broken heart of an Adren who gave their very soul to seal the rift between realms.
