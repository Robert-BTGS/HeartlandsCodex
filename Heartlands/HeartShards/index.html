Heart Shards

The very breath of the cosmos crystallized into form. Their name echoes through every era, whispered in reverent tones by sages and feared by those who’ve glimpsed their true nature. To understand them is to peer into the wound left upon existence itself.

Overview

Heart Shards are fragments of divine will and emotion cast into the mortal plane during the final sacrifice of the Adren. Each shard is unique, pulsing with the intent of its origin. Learn more about the Gods and Primordials tied to their creation.
