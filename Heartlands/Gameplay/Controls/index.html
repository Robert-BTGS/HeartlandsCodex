Controls

VR Controls Quick Sheet

  • Move: left thumbstick
  • Turn: right thumbstick
  • Sprint: click left thumbstick
  • Jump: click right thumbstick
  • Crouch: right thumbstick down
  • Stand: right thumbstick up
  • Grab items: left or right grip
  • Add item to inventory: press A
  • Open inventory: press B
  • Cast spells: left or right trigger

Movement in Heartlands follows common VR patterns:

  • Use the left thumbstick to move.
  • Use the right thumbstick to rotate your view.
  • Click the left thumbstick to toggle sprint.
  • Click the right thumbstick to jump.
  • Push the right thumbstick down to crouch; push it up to stand.
