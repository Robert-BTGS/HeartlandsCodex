Equipment And Holsters

Holsters

You have several holsters on your body: one on each hip, one on each shoulder, and one on the small of your back for shields. To store a weapon, place it near the holster until you feel a vibration, then release. To retrieve it, grab near the holster location.

Quivers

When you equip a bow, your best arrows are automatically placed into a quiver on your back or hip (based on your settings). When you grab an arrow, an outline shows you where to nock it.

Equipping Armor

Open the Armor category, grab a piece, and move it to the appropriate body slot (Head, Chest, Hands, Legs, Feet). To remove armor, grab it off the mannequin.

Weapon Charge And Repair

When a weapon runs out of charge, it returns to your inventory. Recharge it by holding a Heart Shard in one hand and the depleted weapon in the other, then hold the trigger on the hand with the Heart Shard until it is destroyed.

If a weapon breaks, it returns to your inventory. Repair weapons and armor at repair stations in the world.
