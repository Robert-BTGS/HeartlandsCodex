Magic Basics

Magic in Heartlands includes many spell effects and casting styles. Managing your magic resource and choosing the right spell for the moment are key.

Spell Casting Types

Spells can be cast in different ways, such as projectile, placement, channel, and self. See Spell Casting for a full breakdown.

Magic Meter

Keep an eye on your magic meter on your wrist. If you run out of magic, you cannot cast more spells until it refills over time. Recovery speed depends on your Willpower attribute, and potions can restore magic instantly.

Assigning Spells

Open your inventory, go to the Spells category, and grab a spell to assign it to your left or right hand. Hold the trigger to charge, then release to cast.

Spell Scrolls

Spell scrolls are single-use spells. Grab a scroll from the Books section of your inventory and hold the trigger to activate it.
