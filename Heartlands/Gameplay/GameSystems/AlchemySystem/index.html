Alchemy System

  • The alchemy system operates using an alchemy bench, which players must locate in the game world.
  • Alchemy benches are designed to be clearly labeled and easily recognizable.
  • They typically feature a green glow around the bench itself and an adjacent sign displaying a potion icon.
  • It was suggested that a picture of an alchemy bench should be included in the documentation for clarity.
  • The alchemy bench interface is divided into several sections.
  • On the right side of the bench, players will find a list of all their available ingredients.
  • Players can grab ingredients from this shelf and place them into the cauldron situated in front of them.
  • The cauldron is capable of holding a maximum of three ingredients at one time.
  • As ingredients are added to the cauldron, they will be seen floating in jars behind it, with information detailing each of their effects.
  • To successfully create a potion, there must be at least more than one of the combined ingredients that contains the same effect.
  • The strength of the resulting potion is directly determined by the player's current level in the alchemy skill.
  • To complete the crafting process and create the potion, the player must grab the bellows located beneath the cauldron and move them up and down three times to stoke the fire.
  • If a potion is successfully created, it will spawn directly above the cauldron and rotate until the player grabs it.
  • Once obtained, the player has the option to drink the potion immediately or add it to their inventory.
  • If the ingredients added to the cauldron do not produce any usable effects, the crafting process will result in a "failed potion."
