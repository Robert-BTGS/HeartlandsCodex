Fire Rock Golem

Forged in the heart of ancient volcanoes by lost geomancers, the Fire Rock Golem is a towering sentinel of molten fury. Its body is formed from obsidian and basalt plates, fused together by veins of glowing magma that pulse like a heartbeat. With every movement, cracks of fiery light flicker beneath its stony hide, and trails of smoke curl from its joints.

  • Disease Resistance: 100%
  • Fire Resistance: 50%
  • Paralysis Resistance: 30%
  • Poison Resistance: 10%
  • Weakness to Shock: 25%
