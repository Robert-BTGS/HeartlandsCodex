Invisible Pain

Do you ever look for danger? Are you anxious day and night? Probably not if you’re normal and have adapted to your surroundings. I, however, am always anxious and seeking out danger. My brain is always searching for something to be wrong, even when things are great. You could call it a learned behavior of the mind. Why would anyone do this to themselves? It is not by choice, it is inherently engrained since childhood. Such fear and anxiousness also cause physical manifestations. Some say you are cursed by Kaerith, but it has not yet been proven. Oh yes, constant head pain and back pain can plague you from this condition. Due to these manifestations, I have decided to seek out the Saloen monks. I have heard tales of them being great healers in the empire, and perhaps they can help someone like me. This book marks the beginning of my journey to ending this invisible pain and suffering.
