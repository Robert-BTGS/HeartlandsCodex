Binding The Storm

Hecdor would become. He was effective, but cruel: often those facing Hecdor would take much longer to recover from their wounds than any other combatant. He was powerful, but arrogance is no substitute for humility: though it was his talent and skill that saw his success there was no acknowledgement of all those who had helped him achieve his goals. He saw those in the arena only as opponents or tools, never friends. As time continued to march ever forward, it seemed that Hecdor slipped ever backwards. It was far too late to stop the momentum of his descent by the time anyone realized the truth of what occurred. A young man, who lived a life destitute and dull, now found himself surrounded by wealth and power the likes of which he could never have dreamed. He took a new name for himself: Stormbinder. This was the title he fought under for so long. The title that turned a boy into a monster. None who were there will ever forget the horror of his final match. The match that made him arena master. he had been cultivating for many years, and it was ready to bloom. Hecdor fought with a fury that would shake even the core of the gods. When the prior master lay broken at Hecdor's feet, pleading for their life, Hecdor announced the dawn of the blight that has plagued our arena for many years: the blood price. No longer would arena matches be for show, no longer would we spar for exhibition, now was the time of matches fought to the death. The former master was the first who paid this evil tax, and more have followed since. The arena has fallen to the abyss that many others have: the pursuit of wealth at the expense of morals.
