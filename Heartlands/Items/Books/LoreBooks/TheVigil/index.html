The Vigil

People of Rustfall, our world is under siege yet again, this time by the so-called "innovative" field of lithoponics. Let me tell you, this isn't innovation-it's a ticking time bomb. At the heart of this controversy is the vitalite crystals: mystical rocks touted for their ability to emit radiation that makes plants grow. But before we blindly bow down to these "Life Stones" let's take a look at this catastrophe we're welcoming with open arms. First and foremost, we don't have any studies on long term effects of vitalite. We're being asked to trust that these crystals are safe, but where's the proof? What are the long-term impacts of vitalite radiation on plants? I 'd like to see whatever mutant squash these so-called scientists grew, but we've gotten nothing! We've seen this narrative before-introduce a new technology, say it's going to revolutionize its field, and when it inevitably goes wrong, the public -you, me, us- pays the price. What do humans and elves look like 10 years down the road of eating these plants? We need answers, not blind faith. While we're looking at food production, let's not forget the backbone of our food supply: traditional, family oriented farmers. Farmers like my good friend Vonrath. Lithoponics threatens to disrupt centuries-old agricultural practices, like Vonrath's combined 10 generations of experience between him and his family. Imagine how this will affect these hard working farmers and their ability to provide fresh, quality produce and choice cuts of meat to the various markets and restaurants of the greater Rustfall area. This isn't just about new technology-it's about sidelining an entire community that has built up the backbone of society for centuries! We have to stand firm against the reckless adoption of vitalite in farming. We demand comprehensive, long term studies to understand the true impact of lithoponics-because above all, we must prioritize the health and safety of our communities. Don't let yourself be fooled by the glimmer of vitalite. We must stand strong against this threat to our future, our health, our way of life. Don't allow yourself to lose all this for the sake of progress.
