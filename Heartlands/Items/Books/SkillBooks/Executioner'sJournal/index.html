Executioner's Journal

16 Eththo, 212

Today was the first of what I'm sure will be many executions. I thought it would be easy. It always looked so easy when I watched the executioner as a kid. But the cabbages I trained on didn't prepare me for a real beheading. The sickening feedback through the axe as I hit this poor bastard's spine... It felt like snapping a thick parsnip. As my blade carried through his neck, I was reminded why we wear the mask. The thick veins that carry blood to the man's brain released their pressure and coated me in his hot blood. All these sensations hit me at once, and the smell of iron assaulted my nose... I choked my morning's meal back down, and began to clean the blade.
