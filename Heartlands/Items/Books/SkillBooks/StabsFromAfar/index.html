Stabs From Afar

At first glance, this book simply contains drawings of standard attacks and guards that are used while wielding a longsword. Upon further examination, you discover this is a very raunchy romance novel, with the diagrams acting as double entendre and various metaphors for the main characters' sexual exploits.

You get the sense this book was likely passed around by initiates of some religious order, whose belongings are regularly searched. This was likely a way for them to trade means of indulgence without detection from their superiors.
