# Ichpo Atu (The Smiling Islands)

## Jewels of the Sea

* Capital: Israluna

* Land: A chain of volcanic isles draped in tropical jungles, ringed by coral reefs and azure lagoons.

* People: The Antanians, sailors and mystics attuned to the tides and winds, whose songs are said to call storms or calm them.

* Role: The Empire’s naval lifeblood and mercantile hub for exotic goods — spices, pearls, and skyfish drawn from abyssal depths.

* History: Long isolated, the Antanians joined the Empire through pact rather than conquest, binding their sea-gods to imperial faith. Their festivals of light are famed across the coasts, and their pearl-diviners whisper prophecies born of moonlit waters.
