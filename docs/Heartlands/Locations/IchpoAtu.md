# Ichpo Atu (The Smiling Islands)

## The Smiling Islands

* Capital: Israluna
* Dominant Race: [Antanians](../Races/Antanian.md)
* Landscape: Lush tropical islands, coral bays, volcanic islets

## Essence
Scattered like laughter upon the ocean’s breath, the Smiling Islands are a realm of song, spirit, and mystery. The Antanians, catlike and clever, navigate their archipelago with sail and sense, ruled more by consensus than crown. Here, the boundaries between mortal and spirit blur; festivals last for moons, and the wind itself carries omens. Though distant from the imperial throne, their mastery of sea‑trade and spirit‑binding is unmatched.
