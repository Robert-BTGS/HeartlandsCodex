# Locations

All the information about all the locations you might visit in Heartlands.

## Regions
* [Valenmark (Kadia)](Valenmark.md) — The crown of empire and seat of Caelthor.
* [Ichpo Atu](IchpoAtu.md) — Island realm of song, spirit, and sea‑trade.
* [Irgavorn](Irgavorn.md) — Mountain spine of storms and stone‑bound oaths.
* [Virellia](Virellia.md) — Blossoming expanse of forests, renewal, and druids.
* [Tazareth](Tazareth.md) — Shifting edge of salt, wind, and survival.
* [Zircara](Zircara.md) — Verdant shroud of mist, secrets, and serpents.
* [Khurvann (Khurvak)](Khurvann.md) — Bleeding north of ice, honor, and war.
