# Zircara (The Verdant Shroud)

## The Verdant Shroud

* Capital: Ziranu
* Dominant Race: [Nymaa](../Races/Nymaa.md)
* Landscape: Tropical rainforests, mangrove swamps, mist‑drenched deltas

## Essence
Zircara is a realm half‑seen—draped in mist and myth, its secrets guarded by shadow and vine. The Nymaa, serpentkin of sharp wit and ritual grace, rule with a silent elegance. Alchemy, espionage, and the subtle arts flourish beneath the canopy. It is whispered that in Zircara, old gods yet slumber beneath the roots, and the jungle itself decides who lives and who vanishes. Outsiders often find themselves watched… and rarely welcomed.
