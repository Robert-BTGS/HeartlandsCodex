# Zircara

## The Verdant Veil

* Capital:  Ziranu

* Land:  Endless tropical rainforests and mangrove labyrinths, veiled in constant mist and haunted by beasts both sacred and profane.

* People: The Nymaa, fierce and deeply spiritual, whose druids commune with spirits older than empire or even gods.

* Role: Source of rare alchemical reagents, serpent-venoms, and ancient magics drawn from the forest’s heart. Their warriors fight with silent grace, wielding curved blades steeped in ritual blood-oaths.

* History: The Nymaa resisted conquest longer than any province, waging generations of guerrilla war. They swore fealty only after the Pact of Thirel, when the Emperor returned their stolen sacred shard from Mythara’s edge.
