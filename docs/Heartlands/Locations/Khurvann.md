# Khurvann (Khurvak)

## The Frozen Watch

* Capital:  Dravosk

* Land:  Taiga and frozen tundra, where winter reigns eternal and the aurora crowns the night sky.

* People: The Usai, fierce hunters and reclusive mystics, bound by ancestral oaths to guard the world’s northernmost reaches.

* Role: Khurvann is the Empire’s frontier against Mythara’s lurking horrors. Its warriors are famed monster-slayers, wielding obsidian charms against nightmare beasts.

* History: Once fractured into warring tribes, Khurvann united when the first Emperor aided them in banishing the Pale Maw — an ancient Didren beast that devoured stars and souls alike.
