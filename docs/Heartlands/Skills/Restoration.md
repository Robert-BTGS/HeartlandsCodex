# Restoration


Restore, heal, and fortify the very essence of life and magic against any force seeking to do harm.

Where wounds tear, magic binds. Where magic falters, it steadies. Restoration is the art of preserving what endures, flesh, spirit, and arcane flow alike. The spells of this school mend bone, bolster strength, and hold back the grasping hands of death. There are those who think themselves strong, but without restoration, a stab wound is as fatal as venom.

# Perks

* Fortifying Energies: Lvl 10, Absorb spells now have a chance to give magic resistance by 5 percent.
* Keeper of the Cycle: Lvl 20, You deal 20 percent more damage to undead.
* Font of Life: Lvl 30, Healing spells are 25% more effective.
* Apprentice Restoration: Lvl 40, tier 2 spells.
* Circle of Life: Lvl 50, Healing spells cast on player also heals active followers.
* Child of Dawn: Lvl 60, Gain bonus to restoration during the day.
* Journeyman Restoration: Lvl 70, tier 3 spells.
* Pure Vessel: Lvl 80, Slight chance to remove negative status effect when casting restoration spells.
* True Vessel: Lvl 90, Slight chance to resist poison or disease by 10%.
* Expert Restoration: Lvl 100, tier 4 spells.
* Ablation Field: Lvl 110,When an enemy casts a draining or damaging projectile spell time dilates based on static threshold.
* Sun’s Avatar: Lvl 120, While at full health, enemies near you take fire damage.
* Master Restoration: Lvl 125, tier 5 spells.
