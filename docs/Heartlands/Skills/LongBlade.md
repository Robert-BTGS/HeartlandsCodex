# Long Blade


Excelling in reach and tactical control, you dominate in open spaces where your every swing brings devastating slashes.

# Perks

* Proficiency: Lvl 10, Damage from long blades increased by 5 percent.
* Razor’s Edge: Lvl 20, 10% chance to apply bleed when hitting with longsword.
* Steel Flurry: Lvl 30, Attacks from a different direction deal bonus damage, Max 2.
* Powerful Parry: Lvl 40, Parrying with a longsword gives a 15% chance to disarm an opponent.
* Aim for the Gap: Lvl 50, Long blade strikes against unarmored body parts deal +25% more damage.
* Duelist: Lvl 60, While facing 1 enemy you deal 15% more damage and take 10% less.
* Culmination: Lvl 70, The third strike in an alternate direction deals 3x damage.
* Split Focus: Lvl 80, When facing multiple enemies take 25% less damage, but deal up to 10% less damage.
* Brawny: Lvl 90, Negate penalty from using two handed swords with one hand.
* Heft & Precisionn: Lvl 100, Great swords have a 10% chance to ragdoll an enemy. Long swords have a 10% chance to paralyze an enemy for 1 second.
* Might Makes Right: Lvl 110, Two handed blades held in two hands deal 2x damage.
* Sanguine Thirst: Lvl 120, Steal 2% max health on a Steel Flurry Hit.
* Flash Strike: Lvl 125, If you make an attack against an enemy while moving, and they do not hit you, that attack deals 4x damage and immediately applies bleeding.
