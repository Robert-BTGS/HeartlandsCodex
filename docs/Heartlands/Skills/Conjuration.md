# Conjuration


Summon to your side spirits from beyond this world; be they bound to a weapon, animating the dead, or infused with the elements themselves.

A practice as ancient as the gods. To call forces from one place to another, relocate energies across space and time, these are applications of the powers of conjuration. Improper practice risks unstable feedback, or even possession. Mastery, however, offers command over ancient blades, forgotten spirits, and even long dead beasts.

To conjure is to reach into the veil, plunging headlong towards realms unseen and histories long buried. It is the discipline of binding what should not linger: ancestral spirits, elemental forms, spectral blades, or the restless dead. There are those that have misused this power, and suffered possession or worse. Yet when it is mastered, conjuration offers more than mindless spirits and soulless bodies. It offers allies where there were none, and power from a time lost to mortals.

# Perks

* Binding Concentration: Lvl 10, Summons last 10% longer.
* Warding Bond: Lvl 20, While summon is active, you gain 10% magic.
* Arcane Bond: Lvl 30, Slowly gain health when summons are nearby.
* Apprentice Conjuration: Lvl 40, tier 2 spells.
* Ethereal Strikes: Lvl 50, Attacks with summoned weapons ignore a slight amount of opponents armor.
* Soul Trap: Lvl 60, You have a chance of refilling a heart shard when your summoned creature is killed.
* Journeyman Conjuration: Lvl 70, tier 3 spells.
* Guardian Spirit: Lvl 80, Summoned creatures absorb a small amount of damage dealt to you.
* Twinned Soul: Lvl 90, Can summon two creatures or weapons at once.
* Expert Conjuration: Lvl 100, tier 4 spells.
* Persistent Summons: Lvl 110, you gain a slight resistance to damage from spells
* Ethereal Protection: Lvl 120, Summon a matching set of armor when summoning single piece.
* Event Horizon: Lvl 125, When a summon is dismissed, the portal has a chance to suck enemies within a radius into the void.


Extras:

* Summoning: the art of producing creatures, weapons, or armor during a battle.
Summoned weapons reflect damage when parrying.
Summoned creatures are immune to non-silvered attacks.
You can name one summon. It becomes more powerful each time you call it, up to a cap.
Killing an enemy with a summoned weapon has a chance to recharge a heart shard.
