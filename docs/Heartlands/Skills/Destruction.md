# Destruction


Harness the power of natural forces and call elemental wrath on those who stand against you. Be it scorching, shocking, or freezing storms, calamitous ruin is within your grasp.

# Perks

* Arcane Catapult: Lvl 10, Destruction projectile spells travel 200% faster.
* Spell Funnel: Lvl 20, 5% resistance to magic.
* Efficient Channels: Lvl 30, Channeling spells cost 15% less.
* Apprentice Destruction: Lvl 40, tier 2 spells.
* Conduit: Lvl 50, If there is inclement weather, you have a bonus to destruction spells.
* Elemental Aftershock: Lvl 60, Fire spells do burning damage, shock spells have chance to paralyze, frost spells have chance burden.
* Journeyman Destruction: Lvl 70, tier 3 spells.
* Mage Sight: Lvl 80, Destruction placement spells can be cast 200% further away.
* Thunderstruck: Lvl 90, Destruction placement spells have a chance to ragdoll enemies.
* Expert Destruction: Lvl 100, tier 4 spells.
* Interference: Lvl 110, Projectile destruction spells have a chance to half the effectiveness of an enemy’s primary attack skill.
* Overcharged Destruction: Lvl 120, Increase destruction placement spell size by 50%.
* Master Destruction: Lvl 125, tier 5 spells.
