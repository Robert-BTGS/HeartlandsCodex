# Herbology


The study of plants of every variety and the ability to see and bolster the inherent magic in all botanical life.

Herbology is widely considered a preparatory science for potioncraft and reagent refinement. Proficiency enables quick treatment of wounds, foraging of curatives, and identification of toxins in the wild. Skilled herbalists are hard to come by, and indispensable to any settlement.

A study both ancient and essential, Herbology is the careful observation of the natural world’s quiet miracles. Leaves, roots, spores, and sap; each carries purpose, each holds potential. This discipline supports potioncraft and healing, offering knowledge to mend wounds, neutralize poisons, and extract the subtle magic bound within living things. In war or wilderness, skilled herbalists remain rare, and irreplaceable.

# Perks

* Gardening: Lvl 10, Able to plant harvestable ingredients.
* Forager 1: Lvl 20, You no longer have a chance of destroying ingredients when harvesting them.
* Plant Food: Lvl 30, Harvesting a plant has a 5 percent chance to instantly start a fast growth.
* Green Thumb: Lvl 40, Harvestable ingredients you plant have two additional available harvests.
* Forager 2: Lvl 50, You can gather up to 2x ingredients when harvesting.
* Fertilizer: Lvl 60, Plants take 25% less time to regrow.
* Basket Case: Lvl 70, Harvestable ingredients have no weight.
* Forager 3: Lvl 80, You gather up to 3x ingredients when harvesting.
* Efficient Gathering: Lvl 90, Gather in a radius around the player.
* Lithomancer: Lvl 100, Harvestable ingredients you plant have an additional two available harvests.
* Forager 4: Lvl 110, You can gather up to 4x ingredients when harvesting.
* Efficient photosynthesis: Lvl 120, Plants take 50% less time to regrow.
* One with Nature: Lvl 125, Plants the player plants have infinite regrowths.
