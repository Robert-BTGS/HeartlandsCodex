# Nature and Function of Heart Shards

## Nature and Function

Heart Shards are not mere magical batteries. They are crystallized echoes of divine emotion...love, wrath, hope, despair... all layered in complex resonant matrices. Each Heart Shard pulses with the intent of its origin, and thus no two are precisely alike.

* Small shards might enhance spells, hold enchantments, or act as arcane keys.


* Heart Stones can warp reality around them, anchor divine miracles, or even alter the passage of time in localized pockets.
