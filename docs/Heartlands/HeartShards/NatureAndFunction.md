# Nature and Function of Heart Shards

Heart Shards are not mere magical batteries. They are crystallized echoes of divine emotion (love, wrath, hope, despair), layered into complex resonant matrices. Each Heart Shard pulses with the intent of its origin, and no two are precisely alike.

## Uses
* Small shards might enhance spells, hold enchantments, or act as arcane keys.
* Heart Stones can warp reality around them, anchor divine miracles, or even alter the passage of time in localized pockets.
