# Adren Gods

## The Good Gods:

* Gods of Light, Balance, and Benevolence

* God of love and companionship 
Maldara 
Goddess of affection, unity, friendship, marriage, and emotional bonds.

* God of Work & Engineering
Jalar
Patron of ingenuity, builders, tools, and skilled labor.
Brother of Jaral

* God of Logic & Wisdom
Jaral 
Keeper of knowledge, insight, reason, clarity of thought, and scholarly wisdom.
Brother of Jalar

* God of Commerce
Orelian
Oversees trade, wealth, prosperity, and negotiations; honored by merchants and bankers.

* God of Time
Sylador
Oversees fate, history, and the flow of time; worshiped by seers and historians.

* God of Art & Music
Seren
Patron of creativity, inspiration, performers, artists, and poets.

* God of Worldly Pleasure
Mahar
God of worldly pleasures, indulgence, luxury, beauty, and celebration.

* God of Nature and the Heavens
Vaelia
Goddess of the heavens, winds, seas, and elements; protector of natural harmony, weather, storms, and tranquility.

* God of Charity and Luck
Kaelos
God of charity, generosity, luck, fortune, and goodwill; protector of travelers, benefactor of the needy, and guardian of the fortunate.

* God of Justice and Peace
Valdaris
God of justice, peace, law, and moral order
