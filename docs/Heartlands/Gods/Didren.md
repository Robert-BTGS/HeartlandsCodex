# Didren Gods

The Bad Gods:

Gods of Shadow, Discord, and Malevolence


Kaerith
Didren deity of cruelty, torture, pain, and suffering; embodiment of malice, torment, and merciless vengeance.
Realm: Korvaeth
Mythara realm of eternal cruelty, endless torture, and perpetual suffering, echoing with the torment and anguish of lost souls.


Nykareth
Didren deity of tricks, greed, deception, and cunning plots; patron of thieves, manipulators, schemers, and those driven by avarice.
Realm: Velikar
Mythara is a shifting realm of deception, greed, hidden traps, and cunning schemes, where reality twists and betrayal reigns.


Xalrok
Didren deity of chaos, destruction, ruin, and disorder; embodies upheaval, turmoil, and the collapse of structure.
Realm: Xarokai
Mythara of chaos, ruin, disorder, and perpetual upheaval; realm of relentless destruction and eternal turmoil.
Xalrik
Didren deity of strict, oppressive order; enforces absolute discipline, conformity, and ruthless punishment for any deviation.
Realm: Xarikai
Mythara of oppressive order, absolute discipline, strict conformity, and ruthless structure; deviation is swiftly and brutally eradicated.
Malicyn
Didren goddess of curses, malice, hexes, and corruption; mistress of dark magic, and spreading suffering through affliction.
Realm: Vaelikor
Mythara of curses, corruption, and malignant afflictions; a shadowed realm of perpetual blight and poisoned souls.

Morvala
Didren deity of murder, dismemberment, brutality, and worldly pain; revels in violence, bloodshed, and the infliction of mortal agony.
Realm: Dreketh
Mythara of murder, brutality, dismemberment, and worldly pain; a blood-drenched realm defined by eternal violence, agony, and merciless torment.

Jakora
Didren goddess of seduction, infidelity, temptation, and manipulation; mistress of betrayal, lust, forbidden desires, and the corruption of emotional bonds.
Realm: Vaelynis
Mythara of seduction, temptation, infidelity, and corrupted desires; a realm of twisted allure, betrayal, and forbidden passions.

Zhaedria
Didren goddess of darkness, shadows, night, and unseen terrors; mistress of fear, nightmares, secrecy, and hidden evils.
Realm: Umbrakai
Mythara of shadows, darkness, nightmares, and unseen terrors; a realm eternally shrouded in shadow, fear, and hidden horrors.

Pestaris
Didren deity of disease, pestilence, decay, and corruption; spreads plague, rot, illness, and relentless suffering among mortals.
Realm: Malcoris
Mythara of disease, pestilence, decay, and corruption; a realm of perpetual plague, rot, contagion, and endless decay.

Gammorath
Didren deity of madness, insanity, delusion, and fractured minds; lord of mental torment, unpredictable chaos, and derangement.
Realm: Dylaris
Mythara of madness, insanity, delusion, and fractured minds; a realm where reality twists, sanity dissolves, and chaotic visions dominate.


Descriptions you would find in world or maybe even the codex.
