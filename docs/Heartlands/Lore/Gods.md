# Gods

The gods of Heartlands are divided into two great courts: the Adren, stewards of light and balance, and the Didren, forces of shadow and discord. Both shape the world in profound ways.

## The Adren (Gods of Light, Balance, and Benevolence)

They are the stewards of harmony, shepherds of mortal hope. Though their presence is often subtle, their influence binds the Empire in faith and purpose.

### Maldara, Heartbinder
Goddess of affection, unity, and emotional bonds. Her temples host marriages and peace treaties alike. It is said her breath is the wind that softens blades of war.

### Jalar, Forgefather
God of engineering, invention, and physical labor. His hammer shapes not just tools and machines, but civilization itself. Brother of Jaral.

### Jaral, The Silent Watcher
God of wisdom and reason. Cold and calculating, yet never cruel, he guards the great libraries and minds of Erasyl. Scholars call on him with ink and silence. Brother of Jalar.

### Orelian, Coinbearer
God of commerce and prosperity. Ships that sail under his blessing seldom falter. His followers believe that wealth, properly earned, is divine tribute.

### Sylador, Chronarch
God of time and fate. He sees all roads yet walks none. His followers are oracles and timekeepers.

### Seren, The First Song
God of art, music, and dreams. Every masterpiece is a hymn to Seren; every bard, a vessel of his divine melody.

### Mahar, Mistress of Revels
Goddess of pleasure, indulgence, and celebration. To her faithful, joy is a sacred rite. Her festivals light the night skies of Valenmark in fire and fragrance.

### Vaelia, Skywarden
Goddess of nature, weather, and tranquility. Her dominion is the living world, each leaf and stormcloud. In Zircara, jungle priests invoke her name before each monsoon.

### Kaelos, Luckwhisper
God of fortune and travel. A trickster of good humor and divine chance, he aids gamblers, wanderers, and those who take bold steps into the unknown.

### Valdaris, Judgestar
God of law, peace, and justice. His scales weigh kings and commoners alike. His clerics often serve as magistrates throughout the Velassian Empire.

## The Didren (Gods of Shadow, Discord, and Malevolence)

They are the corrosive truth behind all fear. Where the Adren build, the Didren unravel. Not evil in the mortal sense, but forces of entropy, hunger, and raw power.

### Kaerith, The Flayer
God of cruelty, suffering, and punishment. His domain is the scream that follows silence. Worshiped in secret by torturers and warlords.
Realm: Korvaeth — a Mythara realm of eternal cruelty and endless torment.

### Nykareth, Hollow Tongue
God of trickery, greed, and lies. He whispers into contracts and promises, turning them sour. His presence is always felt where trust dies.
Realm: Velikar — a Mythara realm of deception, greed, and hidden traps.

### Xalrok, Worldbreaker
God of chaos and destruction. Every volcano and riot bears his breath. In the Chaos Era, it was said he walked freely, sowing ruin for sport.
Realm: Xarokai — a Mythara realm of chaos, ruin, and perpetual upheaval.

### Xalrik, The Iron Rule
God of oppressive order. The opposite twin of Xalrok: where one brings anarchy, the other enforces suffocating peace.
Realm: Xarikai — a Mythara realm of absolute discipline and ruthless structure.

### Malicyn, Hexmother
Goddess of curses, blight, and malicious sorcery. Her touch withers crops and twists the souls of mages.
Realm: Vaelikor — a Mythara realm of curses, corruption, and malignant afflictions.

### Morvala, Bloodshroud
God of slaughter and murder. His priests wear no names, only blades. In the depths of Khurvann, his cult still chants in the Old Tongue.
Realm: Dreketh — a Mythara realm of murder and endless brutality.

### Jakora, Veil of Thorns
Goddess of seduction and betrayal. She is temptation given flesh, lover and traitor in the same breath.
Realm: Vaelynis — a Mythara realm of twisted allure and forbidden passions.

### Zhaedria, Queen of Night
Goddess of darkness, fear, and unseen things. Children in Virellia are taught to whisper her name when shadows move of their own accord.
Realm: Umbrakai — a Mythara realm of shadows, nightmares, and unseen terrors.

### Pestaris, Rotfather
God of disease and decay. Wherever plague travels, his claw marks remain.
Realm: Malcoris — a Mythara realm of pestilence, rot, and endless decay.

### Gammorath, The Mad Flame
God of insanity and delusion. His voice echoes from shattered minds and false prophets. The Rocca say he dances behind the moon when it wanes.
Realm: Dylaris — a Mythara realm where sanity dissolves and reality twists.
