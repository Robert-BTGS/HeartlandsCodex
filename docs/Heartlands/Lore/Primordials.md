# The Primordials

Before gods, before mortals, before worlds, there existed the Primordials: raw cosmic principles, pure forces of reality. Mortal names barely contain them, but the first and greatest is remembered as Oris, the embodiment of pure existence wandering the infinite Void.

## Oris, Vaoris, and Eloris

In its solitary eternity, Oris began to fracture. From those fractures arose two sentient entities: Vaoris and Eloris. With their birth, Oris faded back into the Void, leaving the two beings alone.

Vaoris embodies change and chaos, yearning for transformation and expression. Eloris embodies order and stability, seeking permanence and structure. Their clashes sparked new ideas, consciousness, and entities, filling the void with complex constructs and ethereal thoughts.

## The Dren and Oravyn

Their struggles gave rise to countless sentient forms known as the Dren, beings of pure thought without physical shape. Seeking coherence, Vaoris and Eloris constructed a new realm called Oravyn and forced the Dren into tangible forms. Having fulfilled their roles, Vaoris and Eloris withdrew silently into the Void, leaving Oravyn to its inhabitants.

Initially furious at their entrapment, the Dren erupted into endless conflict, tearing each other apart only to recombine due to their immortality. After eons of war, a realization took hold: escape from physical form required building new realms within Oravyn and pouring their essence into those creations.

## The Adren and the Didren

The Dren split into two factions. The Didren pursued individual visions, creating countless realms known as Mythara, each reflecting their personal desires and vanity. The Adren united their efforts to create a single grand realm, Erasyl, and populated it with mortal life to house their essences in harmony.

The Didren, envious of the Adren’s achievement, repeatedly assaulted Erasyl. The Adren defended and rebuilt tirelessly.

## The Death of Orathis and the Heart Shards

At a turning point, the Adren discovered how to strip immortality away when a powerful Didren named Orathis attacked Erasyl. They succeeded in killing him, scattering his essence throughout Oravyn. The largest fragment became the moon Thirel.

This act birthed pure magical energy and Heart Shards: fragments capable of containing and focusing this new magic. Large fragments, known as Heart Stones, fell to Erasyl, warping reality and even raising new continents around them.
