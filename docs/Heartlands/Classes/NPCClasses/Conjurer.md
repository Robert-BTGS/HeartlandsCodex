# Conjurer

Conjurers are mages who specialize in bending the veil between realms, calling forth creatures, spirits, and manifestations of raw magic to fight or serve at their command. 

## Favored Attributes
- Intelligence
- Willpower

## Skill Bonuses
- Short Blade
- Alteration
- Sneak
- Conjuration
- Speechcraft
- Destruction
- Illusion
- Restoration

## Specialization
- Magic
