# Greater Sand Golem

Born of desert storms and ancient magic, the Greater Sand Rock Golem towers above mortals like a shifting dune given life. Its body is composed of sun-baked stone and swirling sand, held together by unseen elemental force. When it moves, the sound of grinding rock echoes like thunder across the wasteland.

- Disease Resistance: 100%
- Paralysis Resistance: 70%
- Poison Resistance: 100%
- Shock Resistance: 20%
