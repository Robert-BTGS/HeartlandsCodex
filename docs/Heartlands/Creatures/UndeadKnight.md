# Undead Knight 

The undead knight stands as a grim monument to decay and defiance against death. His once-polished armor now bears a dark green hue, tarnished by centuries of rot and corruption. Faint wisps of sickly light seep from the gaps in his corroded plates, hinting at the unnatural force that animates him. Beneath his visor, two glowing eyes burn like dying embers, casting eerie reflections across the ground.

- Disease Resistance: 100%
- Frost Resistance: 50%
- Paralysis Resistance: 100%
- Poison Resistance: 100%
- Weakness to Shock: 25%
