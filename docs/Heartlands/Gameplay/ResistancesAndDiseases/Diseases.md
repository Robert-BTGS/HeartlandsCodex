# Diseases

### Ash Rot
* Weakens strength by 10%, drains endurance by 15 points

### Fire Lurk
* Reduces magic resistance by 20%, increases fire weakness by 25%

### Sundered Sinew
* Decreases speed by 20%, weapon effectiveness reduced by 15%

### Quivering Plague
* Inflicts 20% weakness to all other disease, damages endurance by 20 points

### Stone Heart
* Reduces strength by 15 points, movement speed slowed by 10%

### Blood Wraith
* Lowers health by 15%, weapon damage output reduced 20%

### Luck’s Bane
* Reduces luck by 25 points

### Grave Chill
* Damages endurance by 10 points, increases frost weakness by 20%

### Wisp Sickness
* Lowers magic pool by 20%, drains willpower 10 points

### Beastclaw Fever
* Reduces strength by 20%, 30% weakness to normal weapons

### Craven Pox
* Damages personality by 15 points, weakens disease resistance by 20%

### Weeping Rage
* Reduces willpower by 15 points, reduces personality by 10 points

### Soul Dropped
* Reduces magic pool by 30 points, increases cost of spells by 10%

### Winter Blight
* Endurance drained by 25 points, slows movement by 20%

### Reaper’s Cough
* 15% vulnerability to poison, reduces health regeneration by 30%, reduces sneak by 20 points

### Crustfall
* Lowers willpower by 20%, lower armor effectiveness by 25%

### Muscle Rot
* Reduces weapon effectiveness by 30%, damages strength by 5 points, damages speed by 5 points, damages endurance by 5 points

### Brain Rot
* Drains intelligence by 15 points, 25% weakness to magic

### Bone Numb
* Reduces speed by 25%, 10% extra damage from blunt weapons

### Vampiric Michosis
* Reduces personality by 25 points, 30% weakness to fire magic

### Venom Vein
* 30% weakness to poison

### Shatter Sickness
* Damages strength by 15 points, 20% weakness to normal weapons

### Life Bane Blight
* Damages health by 20%, 25% weakness to other diseases

### Blaze Blight
* 15% weakness to fire, damages luck by 10 points

### Howling Rift
* Damages personality by 15 points, damages intelligence by 5 points, damages luck by 5 points

### Void Sear
* Damages magic pool by 25%, adds 20% weakness to magic

### Thundershock Plague
* Weakness to shock by 25%, reduces willpower by 20 points

### Ethereal Rot
* Damages willpower, intelligence and personality by 5 points each

### Tundra Blister
* Decreases speed by 20%, frost weakness by 20%

### Wither
* Damage strength, endurance, and speed by 10 points each
