# Diseases

Ash Rot: weakens strength by 10%, drains endurance by 15 points

Fire Lurk: reduces magic resistance by 20%, increases fire weakness by 25%

Sundered Sinew: decreases speed by 20%, weapon effectiveness reduced by 15%

Quivering Plague: inflicts 20% weakness to all other disease, damages endurance by 20 points

Stone Heart: reduces strength by 15 points, movement speed slowed by 10%

Blood Wraith: Lowers health by 15%, weapon damage output reduced 20%

Luck’s Bane: reduces luck by 25 points

Grave Chill: damages endurance by 10 points, increases frost weakness by 20%

Wisp Sickness: lowers magic pool by 20%, drains willpower 10 points

Beastclaw Fever: reduces strength by 20%, 30% weakness to normal weapons

Craven Pox: Damages personality by 15 points, weakens disease resistance by 20%

Weeping Rage: reduces willpower by 15 points, reduces personality by 10 points

Soul Dropped: Reduces magic pool by 30 points, increases cost of spells by 10%

Winter Blight: Endurance drained by 25 points, slows movement by 20%

Reaper’s Cough: 15% vulnerability to poison, reduces health regeneration by 30%, reduces sneak by 20 points

Crustfall: lowers willpower by 20%, lower armor effectiveness by 25%

Muscle Rot: reduces weapon effectiveness by 30%, damages strength by 5 points, damages speed by 5 points, damages endurance by 5 points

Brain Rot: drains intelligence by 15 points, 25% weakness to magic

Bone Numb: Reduces speed by 25%, 10% extra damage from blunt weapons

Vampiric Michosis: reduces personality by 25 points, 30% weakness to fire magic

Venom Vein: 30% weakness to poison

Shatter Sickness: damages strength by 15 points, 20% weakness to normal weapons

Life Bane Blight: damages health by 20%, 25% weakness to other diseases

Blaze Blight: 15% weakness to fire, damages luck by 10 points

Howling Rift: damages personality by 15 points, damages intelligence by 5 points, damages luck by 5 points

Void Sear: damages magic pool by 25%, adds 20% weakness to magic

Thundershock Plague: weakness to shock by 25%, reduces willpower by 20 points

Ethereal Rot: damages willpower, intelligence and personality by 5 points each

Tundra Blister: decreases speed by 20%, frost weakness by 20%

Wither: damage strength, endurance, and speed by 10 points each
