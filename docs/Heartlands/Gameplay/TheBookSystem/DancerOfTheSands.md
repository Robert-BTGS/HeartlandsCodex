# Dancer of The Sands

Near the waters of oasis green
Was found a woman in silken sheen
With skin like brass in forgemaster's fire
She spun her way into my heart's desire

Set with jewels, gilded in gold
Fair dancer warmed desert nights so cold
Swinging and twirling, this dervish drove wild
Rippling razors at those she reviled

* Short Blades
