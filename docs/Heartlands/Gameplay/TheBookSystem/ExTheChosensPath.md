# Ex The Chosens Path

...I trudged unto the place of healing, my axe splintered and my shield crushed to my arm. This was the place for those broken by battle. As Hanndri, who is the great healer of my people, stripped me of my armor and lowered me into those waters of eternal steam and salt... I felt myself change. Those pains suffered from a lost battle were washed away. The shame of loss flowed from my mind. No longer was I a failure. I was born a warrior, and I will continue to fight!
