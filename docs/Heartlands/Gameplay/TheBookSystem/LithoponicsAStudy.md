# Lithoponics A Study

...Though the process of growing flora in controlled environments has been practiced for longer than any person can remember there have been few agricultural advancements as significant as the field of lithoponics. A relatively new field of science, brought on by the discovery of vitalite crystals, lithoponics has led to many unique aspects of agriculture that were previously considered fantasy. First, let us examine some of the unique properties of the catalyst of lithoponics: the vitalite. Vitalite was discovered somewhat by accident. A party of adventurers was descending into what they believed was a recently revealed dungeon. I shan't go into specifics here, but suffice to say they found a large room of thriving plant life previously believed to only be capable of growing on the surface. This room was illuminated by a number of strange crystals, and after some communication with members of the scientific community and some more expeditions, these crystals were officially discovered and given the name vitalite. The most remarkable aspect of these fascinating crystals is one that scientists and arcanists alike are still baffled by: vitalite emits pulses of blue to UV light frequencies. What this means is that vitalite has the ability to function as a light source for small scale agricultural applications. The frequencies of light that are emitted prompt root and leaf growth (making it very good at aiding the growth of various herbs) and the slow pulses of light emitted give enough intermittent activity to simulate a "night cycle" for the plants. Although these traits of vitalite prove incredibly useful and will likely continue to be researched, vitalite is unable to be used in any large scale capacity. As of this writing vitalite cannot be artificially grown, making the procurement of crystals expensive, and it takes many of the crystals to produce viable crops. However, as previously mentioned, vitalite can be used in small scale lithoponics operations where rapidly growing, small scale plants are required. Perhaps the most well executed example of lithoponics exists within the Rustfall arena. The arena staff were able to convert a portion of their infirmary to a small lithoponics wing, enabling certain crucial ingredients to be grown in house. We shall see if in time, the capabilities of such facilities can be developed, and if lithoponics will become a staple of the modern age...
