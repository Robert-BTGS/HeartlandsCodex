# Summoning

* Summonable categories: weapons, armor, and creatures
* Summoning typically spawns the object directly in front of the player character with a particle effect
* Summoned items and creatures have a duration and expire after their time ends
* Player can grab and use a summoned weapon until it expires
* Summoned creatures fight alongside the player until their time expires
* Summon level (quality/strength of weapon, armor, or creature) is determined by the caster’s conjuration skill
* Low conjuration skill limits summons to weaker options (e.g., a rat), higher skill enables magical creatures and stronger gear
* Summoned armor auto-equips immediately upon casting
* When summoned armor expires, the previously worn armor is automatically re-equipped
* If a summoned weapon is dropped or an attempt is made to put it into inventory, it is destroyed immediately.
