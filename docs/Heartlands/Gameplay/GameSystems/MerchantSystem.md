# Merchant System

* Trading requires visiting a merchant with a set-up shop section
* Shop layout: two shelves (left and right), central scales, a set of weights, parchment, ink pot, and feather for signing
* Left shelf: all objects the NPC sells
* Right shelf: all objects the player possesses that can be sold to the NPC
* Display of item values: actual value vs. NPC’s offer value for buying or selling
* Bartering is influenced by the player's mercantile skill
* Mechanics: place an item from the right shelf onto the center scales to see how much gold the NPC will pay
* Mechanics: add an item from the left shelf to the left side of the scales to see the price difference at the bottom
* Use of weights: add to left or right side to decrease or increase the value to balance the deal
* To finalize the deal, sign the parchment
