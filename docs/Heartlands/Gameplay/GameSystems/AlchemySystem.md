# Alchemy System

The art of potion making.

## Recipes

* Restore Endurance potion
Apples and carrots,
Onions and cheese,
Potatoes and pumpkins,
And the rare zucchini.
When boiled together
As a party of three
Bolsters your stomach,
And restores vitality

* Fire Shield potion
With fractured pine I so do light
Matchsticks burnt another night
My mistress’s torch, that crimson fire
Protect me thus, from its ire

* Feather potion
Incense and pearls stole from the sky
Lighten my load, make these old bones spry

* Burden potion
Take a tomato
Paired with a potato
Mix them together
And see how your day goes

* Night Eye potion
Carrots and mushrooms
Throw in fish for a lark
Will give you a potion
To see through the dark

* Detect Life potion
Juice of an orange
Paired with apple sweet wine
Will show you those hearts
Which are bound to thine

* Fortify Marksman potion
It was the foul herb crinila
That saw septum’s fall
This poultice thus begot
The greatest archer of all

* Damage Magic potion
To drain a spellcaster of the font of their power
Simply boil an egg with the anemone flower
Add in a feather to give it a sting
And watch with delight
As you clip the their spells’ wings

* Chameleon potion
An eye for an eye, to mask what is seen
An amethyst deceiver, to fool the most keen
Muddle the light, and hide me from sight

* Restore Health potion
Curds of mushroom and root of beet
Mend my tendons, restore my meat

* Restore Magic potion
Charcoal dust for clarity
And flower that ne’er will die
Gives one power o’er magic
And keeps spells from going awry

* Fortify Health potion
Squash and horn for strengthened bones
Give my body flesh of stone

* “Hoarders strength”
Fortify strength
Feather on self
Potion weighs 0
