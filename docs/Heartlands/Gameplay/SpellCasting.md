# Spell Casting

There are many kinds of spells in Heartlands: spells that deal damage, spells that prevent harm, spells that summon allies, and spells that influence the minds of others. Beyond what a spell does, each spell also has a casting style. Understanding these casting styles will help you choose the right spell for the situation.

## Projectile Spells

Projectile spells launch a bolt or ball of magic toward a target. They are great for direct damage at range, and their effectiveness depends on your aim, distance, and line of sight. Fireballs and frostbolts are classic examples.

## Placement Spells

Placement spells are aimed at a location rather than a target. They have a maximum placement distance and always land where you are looking. Placement spells include several subtypes:

- **Rune Spells**: Place a magical trap that triggers when an enemy approaches.
- **Ground Spells**: Create an effect on the ground that harms or hinders enemies within a radius.
- **Sky Spells**: Call magic down from above to strike an area.

Placement spells are ideal for area control, ambushes, and setting up fights on your terms.

## Channel Spells

Channel spells project a continuous stream of magic, more like a magical flamethrower than a single cast. They are best used when you can keep pressure on a target, but they also require you to stay exposed while channeling.

## Self Spells

Self spells are cast on yourself by throwing the spell upward. They are typically used for recovery or protection, such as healing health or curing poison.
