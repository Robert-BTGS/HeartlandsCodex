# Shield Types

Shields can be used for more than just defense. If an enemy gets close, you can shield bash them to deal a bit of damage and possibly knock them over, giving you a chance to strike.

## Buckler Shields

Buckler shields are small and light. They offer modest protection but are fast to use and stay out of the way. A buckler bash can stagger an enemy, but it won’t hit as hard as larger shields.

## Standard Shields

Standard shields are a balanced choice. They provide solid protection and can deal noticeable damage when you bash. They are large enough to matter in a fight without slowing you down too much.

## Great Shields

Great shields are large and towering. They offer the best protection but are heavy and can make it harder to see. A bash from a great shield is powerful and will almost certainly put an enemy on the ground.
