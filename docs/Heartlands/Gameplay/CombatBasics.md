# Combat Basics

Combat in Heartlands is tactile and physical. Weapons have weight and momentum, so solid swings matter more than light taps. You will need to hit with intent or the weapon may bounce instead of dealing damage.

## Two-Handed Weapons

Some weapons are designed to be used with two hands. They hit harder and can also be used to block, functioning like a shield in a pinch. Larger blades and heavy weapons are the usual candidates for two-handed use.

## Shields And Shield Bash

Shields are more than defense. If an enemy gets close, you can shield bash to deal damage and possibly knock them over, creating an opening. Shield size matters for both protection and bash impact. See [Shield Types](ShieldTypes.md) for details.

## Resistances And Weaknesses

Enemies can have resistances and weaknesses to certain damage types. For example, some enemies resist fire but are weak to frost. Pay attention and adjust your loadout accordingly.

## Looting Fallen Enemies

To loot a fallen opponent, grab their body and an inventory shelf will rise above them. You can then take whatever you want.
