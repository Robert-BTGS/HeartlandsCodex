# Inventory And UI

The inventory and wrist UI are your main sources of information and interaction.

## Inventory Overview

Open your inventory by pressing B on your right controller or grabbing the bag on your hip. The top left shows total carry weight, and the top right shows your gold. Over-encumbered players move more slowly.

The inventory is divided into categories along the bottom:

- Armor
- Weapons
- Books
- Ingredients
- Potions
- Keys
- Misc
- Spells
- Settings and Information

To switch categories, grab the object associated with that category.

## Favorites

Use the Favorites section to access items quickly. Drag an item to the star icon to mark it as a favorite.

## Stats And Information

Open the Settings and Information category to access your stats menu. You can view skills, attributes, quests, and save/load your game. The Information Book is located here (not in the Books category). You can flip pages by sliding your hand across the book.

## Wrist UI

Your wrist UI shows time of day, carry weight, gold, detection status, health, magic, quests, active effects, and level-up readiness. When holding an item, the inner wrist shows item-specific info like value, weight, damage, condition, magical charge, and magic effects.
