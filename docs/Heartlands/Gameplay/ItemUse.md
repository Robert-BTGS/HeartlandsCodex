# Item Use

## Potions

To drink a potion, remove the cork and lift it to your mouth.

## Ingredients

Ingredients are most useful in crafting, but you can also eat them by raising them to your mouth.

## Spell Scrolls

Spell scrolls are single-use spells. Hold the scroll and pull the trigger until the magic forms in your hand.
