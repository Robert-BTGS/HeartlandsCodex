# Rocca

![Rocca](../../images/Rocca.png)

Desert dwellers born into a strict caste system that leaves little room for deviation. Their bodies are well adapted to heat, but isolation in the desert has left them vulnerable to cold and sickness.

**Ancestry:** Human
