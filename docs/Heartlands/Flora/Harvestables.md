# Harvestables and their effects

* Allium: fortify health, restore magic, weakness to poison

* Marsh mallow: cure poison, resist shock, feather

* Hustlewort: restore health, damage magic, fortify personality

* BlueBell: restore health, fortify endurance, damage strength

* Cornflower: restore magic, restore health, restore speed

* Senthowort: fortify personality, fortify speechcraft, damage health

* Rimewort: fortify personality, fortify speechcraft, damage magic

* Magewort: restore magic, fortify magic, fortify long blade

* Daffodil: resist disease, poison, damage heavy armor

* Clubwort: fortify blunt, damage restoration, fortify short blade

* Pale Clubwort: fortify long blade, damage intelligence, fortify unarmored

* Axe Leaf: fortify axe, resist frost, frost shield

* Spellwort: restore magic, fortify herbology, shock weakness

* Violet: damage luck, fortify speed, damage endurance

* Zinnia: restore speed, damage luck, fortify health
