# Letter from a Craftsman

For the last time Hasffan,

I'll not keep buying your leathers if you keep on like you are. Your brine solutions are no good, makes the hides too brittle. There ain't nothing I can do if I try to make a bag or a handle and the leather crumbles under stress. I know times are hard, and I'm asking you not just as a partner, but as a friend: please think about my offer again. If not for your business, for your woman and your little one. I know it's not what you want, but if you can't get the brains and fat for your solutions you'll end up out of work anyways. Just think about it. I don't want to have to find another tanner.

* Light Armor Skill Book
