# The Origins of Magical Healing

The practice of magical healing has been present for as long as the written word. Yet, how has this practice come to be? If we are to believe those more pious than I - who claim the gods have set in motion our perpetual ignorance to keep us from matching their power - why then are we permitted to mend our wounds beyond
our natural medicine? Why then may we reverse the progression of illnesses, regrow lost limbs, and even put a stopper on death? Various healing magics are practiced widely by every culture across the Empire. Yet there is no concensus as to why we are able to heal this way. Saloen monks believe healing comes from channeling abnormal energies caused by sickness or wounds. Various Kadian theologians believe magical healing is an emulation of their own divine heritage, a gift to those born beneath the influence of the gods. Even the various human cultures of the empire have different shamanistic beliefs around the act of healing, often mixing natural healing with chants and rites to invoke spirits. Despite these beliefs, there is no clear answer on how healing came about. While it is usually not a good practice to utilize powers not fully understood, we can assume the generations before us whose of healing has come without significant issues are good enough indications for their continued use.
