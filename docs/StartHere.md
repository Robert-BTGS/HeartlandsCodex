# Start Here

Welcome to the Heartlands Codex, the lore and reference hub for the Heartlands series. The core of the series is exploration, with a focus on making the world feel like a living place that reacts to its history, cultures, and people.

## Series Overview
The Heartlands series is currently in development with three titles:
* Heartlands: The Arena
* Heartlands: The Lost Colony
* Heartlands

## Timeline
All games take place in the Second Era of the world.

Relative to the events of **Heartlands**:
* **Heartlands: The Arena** takes place about 15 years before.
* **Heartlands: The Lost Colony** takes place about 50 years before.

## Canon Policy
Everything in this Codex and in the games is canon unless explicitly labeled otherwise.



## VR Controls Quick Sheet
* Move: left thumbstick
* Turn: right thumbstick
* Sprint: click left thumbstick
* Jump: click right thumbstick
* Crouch: right thumbstick down
* Stand: right thumbstick up
* Grab items: left or right grip
* Add item to inventory: press A
* Open inventory: press B
* Cast spells: left or right trigger
